Terms of Service

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Educational use

CockpitIQ is a browser-based familiarization and recall practice aid. It is not an aircraft checklist, approved training device, flight instruction, or proof of pilot competence. Do not use it to make operational decisions or replace the aircraft's applicable AFM/POH, placards, or instructor guidance.

Accounts and training records

If the account service is configured, you may create an account and save your test results. Keep your password private. Your account shows only your own saved test records. You can delete test records from the dashboard. Contact the project owner through the course faculty to request account removal.

Responsible use

Use the trainer for learning the interface and practicing a simplified sequence. Do not enter private, sensitive, or aircraft-identifying information into the help chat or profile fields.

Availability

The training pages can be used without an account. Account and history features depend on Supabase setup. A result that was not saved to your account is available only for the current session.

Related information

Read the Privacy Notice. For questions about the project, contact its student maintainer through the course faculty.
